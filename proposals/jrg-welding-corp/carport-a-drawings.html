General Notes

  • All aluminum welding with 5356 filler by certified aluminum welder; 1/4" fillet welds unless noted.
  • All bolts, nuts, washers and anchor rods stainless steel Type 316. Bolt holes 1/16" oversize, deburred.
  • Galvanized deck bearing directly on aluminum is a compatible (zinc) contact; all deck cut edges touched up with cold-galvanizing compound.
  • Footing tops poured flush with grade, smooth and level. Anchors installed into cured concrete (3,000 psi, min 7 days) with Hilti-specified bit and torque — screw-in anchors only, no expansion anchors. Isolation barrier (bituminous paint or membrane pad) between aluminum plate and concrete.
  • Layout, post locations and final grades field-verified with owner before excavation. Roof deck overhangs the frame 1'-0" on all four sides. Deck sheet lengths confirmed against as-built frame before ordering.
  • Structure designed as open (no walls). Do not enclose sides without review — wind loads change.
