PROJECT
Replace the wood railings on both elevated porches with a custom Chippendale-pattern aluminum railing, designed and welded by JRG Welding (±200 LF, 42" high) — an exclusive, permanent, maintenance-free piece.

1. Existing Conditions

The wood railings on both elevated porches are weathered and loose, with split cap boards and a heavy, closed picket look. Approximately 200 linear feet of railing total (both decks, approx. 26' × 50' each), guard height 42".

2. Proposed Solution — Custom Chippendale Aluminum Railing, 42"

  • Exclusive design, fabricated by JRG Welding: the classic Chippendale fretwork pattern selected by the owner, shop-welded panel by panel in aluminum — not a catalog product; a one-of-a-kind piece made for this home.
  • Construction: 1"×1" aluminum tube frames with 3/4" aluminum pattern bars, welds ground smooth; posts at less than 4'-0" on-center per guardrail code — one pattern module per panel.
  • Powder-coat finish in the owner's color (classic white as the reference photo, or any standard color) — never rots, never splinters, never repainted.
  • Code-compliant: 42" guard height, 4" sphere rule respected in the pattern, engineered post anchoring to the deck framing.

3. Scope of Work

  1. Demolition: Remove the existing wood railings on both porches completely (posts, pickets and caps); haul away and legally dispose.
  2. Fabrication & installation: Shop-weld the custom Chippendale panels and posts in aluminum per the approved shop drawing; powder coat; install with posts anchored to the deck framing with structural hardware — sections level, rigid, corners and terminations finished clean.
  3. Closeout: Shake-test every section, touch-up, cleanup and walkthrough with owner.
