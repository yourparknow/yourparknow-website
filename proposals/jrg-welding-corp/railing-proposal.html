PROJECT
Replace the wood railings on both elevated porches with a 42"-high powder-coated aluminum railing system (±200 LF) — permanent, maintenance-free, open modern profile.

1. Existing Conditions

The wood railings on both elevated porches are weathered and loose, with split cap boards and a heavy, closed picket look. Approximately 200 linear feet of railing total (both decks, approx. 26' × 50' each), guard height 42".

2. Proposed Solution — Powder-Coated Aluminum Railing, 42"

  • Permanent: aluminum does not rot, splinter, rust or feed termites — no more cap boards to replace, ever.
  • Factory powder-coat finish (dark bronze or matte black) — zero painting, zero maintenance.
  • Open, modern profile — replaces the heavy wood pickets with a clean look that opens the view of the property.
  • Code-compliant: 42" guard height, 4" sphere rule, engineered post spacing, stainless/coated structural anchors to the framing.

3. Scope of Work

  1. Demolition: Remove the existing wood railings on both porches completely (posts, pickets and caps); haul away and legally dispose.
  2. Installation: Furnish and install the selected 42" aluminum railing system — posts anchored to the deck framing with structural hardware, sections level and rigid, corners and terminations finished with manufacturer components.
  3. Closeout: Shake-test every section, touch-up, cleanup and walkthrough with owner.
