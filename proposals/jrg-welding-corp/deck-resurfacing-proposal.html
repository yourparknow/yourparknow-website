PROJECT
Complete deck re-surfacing of both elevated porches (±2,600 SF) in capped composite deck boards — rot-proof, splinter-free, 25-year warranty, no maintenance.

1. Existing Conditions

The wood deck boards on both elevated porches (front and rear, approx. 26' × 50' each — ±2,600 SF total) are weathered, checked and at the end of their service life. The framing below is being renewed under the structural hardware contract currently in progress.

2. Proposed Solution — Capped Composite Decking

  • Veranda® capped composite deck boards (manufactured by Fiberon), 1"×6" grooved-edge profile — a polymer-protected surface that does not rot, does not splinter, and feeds no termites.
  • 25-year manufacturer warranty — essentially zero maintenance: no sanding, no sealing, no painting, ever.
  • Wood-look finish; color selected by owner (gray or brown).
  • Hidden fastener system — clips between boards, no screw heads visible on the walking surface.

3. Scope of Work

  1. Demolition: Remove all existing wood deck boards on both porches; haul away and legally dispose of debris.
  2. Framing check: Verify joist spacing and condition (composite requires joists at 16" on-center maximum). Any blocking, sistering or additional joists required is reported and quoted by written change order before installation.
  3. Installation: Install capped composite deck boards over the renewed framing with the manufacturer's hidden fastener system, correct gapping for expansion, staggered joints, and picture-frame/finish cuts at edges and openings.
  4. Closeout: Walk both decks board by board, final cleanup and walkthrough with owner.
