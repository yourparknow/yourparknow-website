PROJECT
Railing and stair renewal at both elevated porches (front and rear): new full-length 2×6 railing caps, complete railing re-alignment and re-fastening, and full rebuild of both five-step stair flights.

1. Existing Conditions

At both elevated porches (front and rear): the 2×6 railing cap boards are split and weathered with open joints and exposed rusted nails; the railing assemblies have worked loose and are out of plumb; and both five-step stair flights have treads and framing at the end of their service life.

2. Scope of Work

  1. Railing caps — both porches (~200 LF): Remove the existing split cap boards and install new full-length select pressure-treated 2×6 top caps on both elevated deck railings, fastened with exterior-grade structural screws (no nails), joints cut at 45° over posts and sealed — no open joints, no exposed fasteners.
  2. Railing re-alignment & re-fastening: The railing assemblies are loose and out of plumb. Plumb, straighten and re-secure the complete railing runs on both porches — posts re-aligned, sections drawn tight, and every connection re-fastened with structural screws so the railings are rigid, with no movement.
  3. Stair flights — demolish & rebuild (both): Demolish the existing five-step stair flight at the front porch and at the rear porch. Furnish and install new pressure-treated ground-contact stringers, new treads and galvanized stringer connectors; stairs and adjoining railing temporarily supported, jacked and shimmed as required during the work; new caps on the stair rails.
  4. Disposal: Haul away and legally dispose of all demolished material.
  5. Closeout: Walk every tread and every foot of railing, verify plumb and solid, cleanup.
