PROJECT
Deck stair re-treading — replace the weathered, cracked treads on the five (5) step deck stair with new material; existing stringers remain.

1. Existing Conditions

The five (5) step wood stair at the deck has treads at the end of their service life — deeply checked, cracked and weathered boards. The stair stringers and supporting structure are in serviceable condition and remain.

2. Scope of Work

  1. Demolition: Remove the five (5) worn treads; haul away and dispose of debris.
  2. Stringer check: With the treads off, inspect the stringers. If rot is found in the stringer notches, a stair rebuild is quoted separately by written change order before proceeding.
  3. New treads: Install new pressure-treated treads, cut to fit, fastened with exterior-grade structural screws, edges eased and aligned.
  4. Closeout: Walk the stair, verify every tread solid, cleanup.
