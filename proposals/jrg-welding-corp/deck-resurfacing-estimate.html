CONCEPT UNDER CONSIDERATION
Replace all wood deck flooring on both elevated porches (approx. 26' × 50' each — ±2,600 sq ft total) with capped synthetic (composite/PVC-capped) deck boards.

1. What This Covers

Budget-level pricing to remove the existing wood deck boards on both elevated porches and install new capped synthetic deck boards — a polymer-protected surface that does not rot, splinter or feed termites, with a 25+ year manufacturer warranty and essentially zero maintenance. Three quality levels are shown so the owner can match the project to his budget. All figures include demolition, disposal, materials, hidden-fastener installation and labor.
