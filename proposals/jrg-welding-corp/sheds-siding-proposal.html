PROJECT
Exterior re-cladding of two (2) storage sheds with James Hardie fiber-cement panel siding, plus new exterior doors (one double, one single) — rot-proof, termite-proof.

1. Existing Conditions

Two (2) storage sheds on the property have exterior wood panel cladding with advanced rot deterioration, concentrated at the bottom of the panels where they meet the ground. The walls are 8'-0" high. Shed 1 (12' × 12'): three walls are re-clad — the rear wall is protected by its covered porch and remains; its front double door is replaced. Shed 2 (10'-2" × 10'-2"): all four walls are re-clad and its single entry door is replaced.

2. Proposed Solution — Fiber-Cement Panel Siding

Re-clad the shed walls with James Hardie HardiePanel HZ10 vertical siding, Sierra 8 wood-grain texture, 4'×8' sheets, 5/16" thick fiber-cement — the HZ10 formulation is engineered specifically for hot, humid climates like South Florida:

  • Rot-proof and termite-proof — cement-fiber composition; nothing to decay, nothing for insects to eat.
  • Weather resistant — engineered for Florida sun, rain and wind; fire resistant.
  • Wood-grain Sierra 8 texture with vertical grooves — the finished look of wood siding without its problems.
  • Factory-primed finish, ready for paint. Painting is NOT included in this proposal — final paint color and painting by owner.

3. Scope of Work (each shed)

  1. Demolition: Remove only the rotted exterior wall boards — the existing 2×4 stud wall framing is in good condition and remains in place as the structure the new panels fasten to. Haul away and legally dispose of debris.
  2. Framing inspection: With the walls open, inspect the studs and plates. Sound framing is re-used; any rot or termite damage found in the framing is reported to the owner and repaired only under an approved written change order before closing the walls.
  3. Moisture barrier: Install weather-resistive wrap over the framing on all re-clad walls.
  4. Panel installation: Install HardiePanel HZ10 Sierra 8 5/16" fiber-cement panels (James Hardie), fastened to the existing 2×4 studs with corrosion-resistant fasteners per James Hardie installation requirements, maintaining the required clearance from grade and concrete (the reason the current panels rotted — the new siding will NOT touch the ground); flashing at tops and corners; joints sealed with exterior sealant; trim at corners and openings.
  5. Exterior doors: Furnish and install new exterior doors rated for outdoor humidity and heat (fiberglass or steel, Masonite / JELD-WEN or equal — no rust, no rot, no warping): Shed 1 — one (1) double door 72"×80" (both leaves) at the front elevation, and Shed 2 — one (1) single door 36"×80"; complete with exterior-grade hinges, lockset and deadbolt, astragal and flush bolts at the double door, weatherstripping and sweeps, sealed and flashed. Doors delivered factory-primed — final paint by owner.
  6. Prime & seal: All panel cut edges and joints sealed; siding, trim and doors left in factory-primed finish, ready for the owner's painter. Painting is not included.
  7. Closeout: Final cleanup and walkthrough with owner.
