PROJECT
Elevated deck hardware renewal — two (2) identical porch structures: all new connectors and fasteners, railing-fascia reinforcement, eight (8) joists and the long column tie rods replaced.

1. Existing Conditions

The two (2) identical elevated porch/deck structures at the residence show advanced corrosion in the structural hardware: galvanized joist hangers, framing angles and post brackets are rusted, and the long galvanized all-thread rods that tie the framing to the main columns are oxidized. The fascia boards where the railings are mounted have worked loose. In addition, eight (8) deck joists show deterioration and require replacement. The remaining framing lumber is sound.

2. Scope of Work (both structures)

  1. Connectors — ALL NEW, with ALL NEW NAILS: Remove and replace every joist hanger, framing angle/clip and post bracket on both deck structures with new heavy-galvanized ZMAX / G185 (triple-zinc) connectors rated for exterior use and treated lumber. Every connector is installed with all-new hot-dip galvanized structural nails — no old nails or fasteners are re-used anywhere. Estimated count: approximately 230 connectors total (final count field-verified).
  2. Railing-fascia reinforcement: The fascia boards where the railings are mounted are loose. Each railing-mount fascia will be reinforced and re-secured with additional structural screws through to the framing, and all railing connections tightened — railings left firm, no movement.
  3. Long column tie rods — replace ALL: Remove the long rusted galvanized all-thread rods that tie the joists/framing against the main support columns, and replace with new hot-dip galvanized all-thread rods with new galvanized nuts and washers, re-tensioned.
  4. Joist replacement: Remove and replace eight (8) deteriorated deck joists with new pressure-treated lumber matched to the existing size and length; deck boards carefully lifted and re-fastened; new hangers with new nails at each replaced joist included in the connector count.
  5. Sequence & safety: Connectors replaced one at a time — the structure remains fully supported at all times.
  6. Closeout: Final inspection of every connection, cleanup, and walkthrough with owner.
