PROJECT
Two (2) free-standing open carport structures in welded structural aluminum with galvanized steel roof deck: Carport A — 24' × 25' (two vehicles) and Carport B — 16' × 20' (compact tractor).

1. Design Concept

Both structures are open-sided (no walls), fully welded structural aluminum frames — no rust, no rot, no painting ever required — covered with galvanized steel roof deck mechanically fastened to the frame. Columns are embedded in individual concrete footings; the embedded-column system resists wind without any diagonal bracing, keeping the structures completely open and clean. Roofs are single-slope (approx. 1 : 12) for positive drainage.

Carport A is designed per the owner's request with no intermediate posts and no interior columns — the full 24-foot width is spanned by deep aluminum girders (3"×12"), leaving both parking bays completely unobstructed.

2. Carport A — 24' × 25' (Two Vehicles)

  • Columns: Eight (8) aluminum posts 4"×4"×1/4" 6061-T6 — four per side, aligned under each roof girder; no posts at front, rear, or interior. Clear height approx. 9'-0" at the high side, sloping to approx. 7'-0".
  • Roof girders: Four (4) aluminum girders 3"×12"×0.188" 6005A-T61, spanning the full 24' width (front, rear, and two intermediate at 8'-4" on center), through-bolted to the columns with stainless hardware.
  • Perimeter side beams: 3"×6"×3/16" 6061-T6 continuous along both 25' sides, closing the perimeter frame and tying all columns together.
  • Roof deck: Galvanized steel roof deck, 1-1/2" Type B, 18 gauge, in continuous full-length sheets over three spans, fastened at every support with 1/4"-14 self-drilling fasteners and stitched at side laps.
  • Foundations: Eight (8) concrete footings 2'-0" × 2'-0" × 3'-0" deep; columns embedded 3'-0" with stainless steel cross-pin anchors; embedded aluminum protected with bituminous coating per code.

4. Scope of Work (both structures)

  1. Layout & excavation: Stake both structures, excavate fourteen (14) footings 2'×2'×3'-0" deep.
  2. Fabrication: Cut and weld all aluminum framing (certified aluminum welding); girders and beams drilled for stainless through-bolt connections; embedded column sections coated with bituminous protection.
  3. Erection & concrete: Set columns plumb and braced to exact grade, pour 3,000 psi concrete footings crowned for drainage; install girders, side beams and rafters after cure.
  4. Roofing: Install galvanized deck with self-drilling fasteners at every support and side-lap stitching; edges finished clean — no sharp exposed corners.
  5. Closeout: Site cleanup, haul-away of spoils and debris, final walkthrough with owner.
